# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 1 June 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Pinterest Auto-Publisher** application
("the App") handles data. The App is a personal, single-user automation tool
that publishes its owner's own content to the owner's own Pinterest account
through the official Pinterest API.

## Who operates the App

The App is operated privately by an individual content creator (the "Operator").
Contact: **listosha@gmail.com**.

## What the App does

The App reads a list of the Operator's own posts (titles, descriptions, links,
and images that the Operator created) and publishes them as Pins to the
Operator's own Pinterest boards on a schedule, using the Pinterest API v5.

## Data we access

To function, the App uses an OAuth access token issued by Pinterest to the
Operator's own Pinterest account. With this token the App may:

- create Pins and read/list the Operator's own boards;
- read basic account information needed to target the correct board.

The App acts **only on the Operator's own account**. It does not access, read,
collect, or store data belonging to any other Pinterest user.

## Data we collect from other people

**None.** The App has no end users other than the Operator. It does not have a
sign-up flow, does not collect personal data from visitors, and does not track,
profile, or build audiences from other people. It serves no third parties.

## How tokens are stored

OAuth tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the Operator's own automation
environment (GitHub Actions encrypted secrets) and are used solely to call the
Pinterest API. Tokens are never shared, sold, or transmitted to any third party
other than Pinterest itself.

## Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. The only external
service the App communicates with is the official Pinterest API.

## Data retention and deletion

The only stored credentials are the OAuth tokens described above. The Operator
can revoke the App's access at any time from Pinterest account settings, after
which the tokens become invalid. No other personal data is retained.

## Children

The App is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone other than
the Operator.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated; the "Last updated" date above reflects the latest
revision.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: **listosha@gmail.com**.
